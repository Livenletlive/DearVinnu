Dear You ❤️

Baby...

I let my anger speak before my heart did.

and argue later.

this is my tiny way of fixing things.

when I'm secretly missing you.

I know I overthink.

I know I let my emotions win sometimes.

I'm still learning...

how to love you without letting my anger speak for me.

The truth is...

even when I was angry...

I still wanted to tell you about my day.

I still wanted to hear your voice.

I still wanted you...

Nen kuda possessive avta ankoled baby... But here I am. ❤️

your heart doesn't know how to stop missing them.

this little website won't magically fix everything.

But...

I hope it reminds you of one thing.

Behind all my overthinking...

all my stubbornness...

and all my unnecessary drama...

there's a girl...

who simply...

loves you.

Very. Very. Much. ❤️ If you're smiling right now... then this little website did exactly what I hoped it would. ❤️
